## English

This repo contains a basic GitHub Page ([benatgaldos.net](https://benatgaldos.net)), available in Spanish and English, that summarises my work in Data Governance, Data Products and Palantir Foundry, and links to my professional profiles, to [my personal blog](https://www.bennytacora.com/en/)
that I update every sometimes, and to the products of [Galde](https://galde.app/), the company I founded.

It also reflects my time at [Global Shapers](https://www.globalshapers.org/), a community powered by the [World Economic Forum](https://www.weforum.org/) that was a highlight of my career for almost 10 years and of which I am now an Alumni.

Fonts and icons are served from this repo (see `fonts/README.md`); icons are from [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com/license/free) (CC BY 4.0).

Special thanks to [Iñigo Lopez-Gazpio](https://github.com/lgazpio/lgazpio.github.io) and [Dinesh Pandiyan's design](https://github.com/flexdinesh/dev-landing-page) to inspire me to build this mini-repo.
